The kitchen

Nothing catered.
Everything 112.

Most event venues in Miami Beach warm something that was cooked elsewhere. Prime Private has a full standalone kitchen inside the building, and Prime 112's chef is in it on the night.

The dry-aged program. The raw bar. The sides. Not a version of them — the same kitchen, thirty feet from the table.

And because that kitchen belongs to the venue alone, it can be kashered for your event. Full kosher service, cooked on site by the same team — not sent in from a caterer in foil trays. A restaurant sharing its kitchen with a working dining room cannot do this at all, which is why almost nobody at this level offers it.
